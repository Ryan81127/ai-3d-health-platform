  Original project: http://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/
  STL conversion: https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D

Changes made: meshes were simplified (triangle reduction), the skin mesh was reduced
to its outer surface, coordinates were converted to metres with Y up, parts were
grouped into layers, and files were compressed (glTF + meshopt). See scripts/build-model.py.
