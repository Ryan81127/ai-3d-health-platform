3D model files in this directory and their licences
====================================================

1) skin.glb, muscle.glb, skeleton.glb, organ.glb, vessel.glb
   Derived from BodyParts3D 3.0 (STL conversion: https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D)
2) bp4-vessel.glb (limb arteries and veins), bp4-eye.glb (cornea, iris, lens, sclera, vitreous body,
   eyelid tarsal plates, lacrimal gland and drainage)
   Derived from BodyParts3D 4.0 (partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip, https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html)

   Attribution: "BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science"

   To comply with both, these derived files are attributed as above AND distributed under the same
   share-alike terms (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP / compatible later versions).

3) za-nerve.glb (spinal cord and peripheral nerves), za-organ.glb (thyroid gland, tongue)
   Derived from Z-Anatomy FBX models (https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy/tree/PC-Version/Resources/Models/FBX)
   Attribution: "Z-Anatomy - The open source atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"

   (licence not stated), cranial nerves (University of Dundee, CC BY 4.0, not used here).

Changes made (all files): triangle reduction, skin reduced to its outer surface, unit conversion to
metres with Y up, alignment to a common body frame (Z-Anatomy aligned by organ centres, ~1 cm error),
grouping into layers, glTF + meshopt compression. See scripts/build-model.py, build-bp4.py, build-za.py.

These licences cover the model files only, not the application code or the health-education text.
